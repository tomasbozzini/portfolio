## Problem

University project: explore a navigation approach that combines classic reactive control with a language model's decision-making.

## Solution

A Pioneer robot simulated in Webots and controlled from Python. The reactive layer follows the Braitenberg vehicle model; on top of it, an LLM served by Groq takes part in the navigation decisions.
