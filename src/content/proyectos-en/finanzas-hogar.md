## Problem

Keeping the household accounts in one shared place, without scattered spreadsheets, and being able to see where the money goes each month.

## Solution

A web app to log expenses and income by category, with fixed expenses, a savings fund kept apart from the rest, pie charts by category and month-over-month comparison. We use it together at home.
